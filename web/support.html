Warmer Support

Warmer lets blind and low-vision people use touchscreens that don't talk, like restaurant kiosks. Point your phone's camera at the screen, hold the big button and say what you want. Warmer guides your finger to each button with vibrations and short spoken directions.

Getting started

  1. Hang the phone on a chest lanyard or put it on a stand, with the back camera facing the touchscreen.
  2. Hold the big button and say what you want, for example "a cheeseburger and fries".
  3. Move one finger over the screen. The phone ticks faster as you get closer. When you are on the button, you feel one long buzz and hear "Press."
  4. Press, and Warmer guides you to the next button until you're done.

Tips

  • A small blue or pink sticker on your fingernail helps Warmer follow your finger.
  • Keep the whole screen in view of the camera, and avoid strong glare.
  • Tap Repeat to hear the last instruction again, or Retry to read the screen again.
