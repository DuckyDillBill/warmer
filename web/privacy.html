Warmer Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

Warmer helps blind and low-vision people use touchscreens. To do that, it has to see the screen in front of you and hear what you want. This page explains exactly what leaves your phone, where it goes, and what is kept.

What Warmer sends, and why

  • Photos of the screen. While an order is in progress, Warmer takes photos with your phone's camera and sends them to our backend and to Google's Gemini AI, which reads the buttons and decides which one to guide you to.
  • Your voice. When you hold the big button and speak, the recording is sent to our backend and to Google's Gemini AI to turn it into text.
  • Your answers and order steps. What you asked for, your answers to questions like "Make it a meal?", and the buttons you pressed are sent to our backend so Warmer can plan the next step.

Warmer only asks for this after you agree on the first screen of the app.

What is kept

  • We do not keep the photos or voice recordings after they are processed.
  • Our backend keeps a short text record of each order (what you asked for and the steps taken) and text descriptions of the screens it read (button names and positions). We use these to fix problems and improve Warmer.

What never leaves your phone

  • Your PIN. You type it into a secure field on your phone. Warmer never says it out loud, never logs it, and never sends it anywhere. It is cleared when the order ends.

Who processes your data

  • Google (Gemini API) reads the screen photos and your voice. Google processes them under its Gemini API terms.
  • Convex hosts our backend.
  • Vercel hosts the camera page the app loads, and may keep standard web server logs.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • We never sell your data.

Children

Warmer is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

You can stop at any time by closing the app, and you can turn off camera or microphone access in your iPhone's Settings. To ask us to delete the order records described above, email ashkon.chaghajerdi@berkeley.edu.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
